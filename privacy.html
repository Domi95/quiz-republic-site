Privacy Policy

Quiz Republic · Last updated November 24, 2026

Quiz Republic is a daily trivia game made by Domagoj Beronić, an individual developer in Croatia (“I”, “me”). This page explains what the app collects, why, who else handles it, and how you can delete it.

In short

  • Your account and game progress are stored so your rating, streak and friends work.
  • Anonymous usage statistics help me decide what to improve. You can turn them off in Settings.
  • No ads. Your data is never sold, and you are not tracked across other apps or websites.

What the app collects

Account

When you sign in with Apple or Google, the app receives an account ID and your email address. With Sign in with Apple you can hide your email behind a relay address. Nothing else from those accounts is kept.

Game data

The quizzes you played, which answers were right or wrong in each category, your rating and its recent history, and your streak.

Friends and groups (only if you use them)

Your nickname, your friend code, the players you follow, and the groups you create or join (group name and members). Anyone who enters your code, and everyone in your groups, sees your nickname, rating and streak. Your email is never shown to other players.

Usage statistics

Through Google Analytics for Firebase: which screens you open and which features you use (for example a quiz answered, its category and how long it took, a friend added, a result shared), plus device model, iOS version, app version, app language and an approximate location (country or region) worked out from your IP address. This is tied to a random ID for the app on your device, not to your account or email. You can turn it off at any time in Settings → Share usage data.

Crash reports

If the app crashes, Firebase Crashlytics sends technical details (device, iOS version, app version and where in the code it crashed), tied to the same random app ID.

App configuration

Firebase Remote Config checks whether your app version is still supported.

Question images

Images in questions load from jsDelivr, a public content network, which sees your IP address like any website you visit.

Messages to me

If you email me (Contact us, or reporting a nickname or group name), I receive your email address and your message.

What the app does not do

  • No advertising, and no sharing of your data for advertising.
  • No advertising identifier (IDFA) and no tracking across other companies’ apps or websites.
  • No access to your precise location, contacts or photos.

Why it is used

  • To run the game you signed up for: your account, rating, streak, friends and groups.
  • To keep it fair and safe: handling reports of offensive nicknames or group names.
  • To improve the app: usage statistics and crash reports. You can opt out of usage statistics in Settings.

Who handles your data

  • Google (Firebase): sign-in, the game database, usage statistics, crash reports and app configuration. The game database is stored in the EU; other Firebase services may process data in the US. Firebase privacy
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple. Apple privacy
  • Google: Sign in with Google. Google privacy
  • jsDelivr: question images. jsDelivr privacy

How long it is kept

  • Account, game, friends and groups data: until you delete your account.
  • Usage statistics: for a limited period set in Google Analytics, then deleted automatically.
  • Crash reports: 90 days.
  • Emails: as long as needed to answer or handle a report.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your account, rating, stats, nickname and friend code, and takes you out of every group. Usage statistics are not tied to your account; turn them off in Settings and they stop at once. For anything else, email me and I will delete it.

Children

Quiz Republic is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has signed up, email me and I will delete the account.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or object to how it is used, by emailing me. If you are in the EU, you can also complain to your data protection authority; in Croatia that is AZOP.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page is updated and the date at the top changes with it.
